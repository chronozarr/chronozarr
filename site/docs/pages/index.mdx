<section className="cz-grid cz-section cz-rule" aria-label="What a store does">
  <p className="cz-label">What a store does</p>
  <ol className="cz-capabilities">
    <li><h2>Plays like a video</h2><p>Scrub or play years of observations. The viewer loads the timesteps around the current one in the background.</p></li>
    <li><h2>Zooms and pans</h2><p>A pyramid of coarser levels keeps a view of the whole area to a few downloads. Level 0 is your data at full resolution.</p></li>
    <li><h2>Shows pixel values</h2><p>Click a pixel to chart its values over time, in physical units. Products such as NDVI are computed from the stored bands.</p></li>
    <li><h2>Shares the view</h2><p>A link holds the date, the product, the display range, the zoom and the map centre. The recipient needs only a browser.</p></li>
    <li><h2>Keeps the numbers</h2><p>xarray, zarr-python and GDAL read the same files and return the stored values. The viewer draws the same values.</p></li>
    <li><h2>Needs no server</h2><p>A store is static files. Serve it from your computer for a temporary link, or from your own cloud storage for a permanent one.</p></li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section className="cz-grid cz-section cz-rule" aria-label="Three commands">
  <p className="cz-label">Three commands</p>
  <div className="cz-commands">
    <pre><code>{`chronozarr convert rasters/ my_store
chronozarr share my_store
chronozarr preview my_store`}</code></pre>
  </div>
  <div className="cz-commands-notes">
    <p><code>convert</code> reads one GeoTIFF per date and checks every file before it writes. <code>share</code> prints a temporary link that a colleague can open. <code>preview</code> opens the store in your browser, on this computer only.</p>
    <a href="/quickstart">Quickstart, with a sample dataset</a>

<section className="cz-grid cz-routes cz-rule" aria-label="Two ways to share">
  <p className="cz-label">Two ways to share</p>
  <div className="cz-route cz-route-wide"><h2>Temporary link</h2><p><code>chronozarr share</code> serves the store from your computer through a Cloudflare quick tunnel. The link works while the command runs and your computer is on. Each view is sent over your upload connection. Anyone with the link can read the store. No account is needed.</p><a href="/guides/share">Share a temporary link</a></div>
  <div className="cz-route cz-route-wide"><h2>Permanent link</h2><p><code>chronozarr publish</code> uploads the store to Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, Google Cloud Storage or Azure, in your own account. It checks the hosted copy and prints the link. You pay your provider for storage and downloads. chronozarr.org serves the viewer page and stores no data.</p><a href="/guides/publish">Publish a permanent link</a></div>
</section>
